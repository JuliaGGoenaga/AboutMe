MUN Refugee Challenge

A 3 by 10 metre backdrop for the General Assembly of MUN Bilbao 2020, part of the United Nations MUN Refugee Challenge. The image shows the reality of refugees as two sides of the same coin, and asks the viewer to look at it from the other side.
