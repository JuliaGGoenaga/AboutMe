COLAB

Intergenerational centre. Master’s final project

Place
Spain
Context
Master's in Architecture and Sustainability

A centre where different generations share space and activity. Developed from the basic design to the execution project.

  • Basic design (PDF)
  • Execution project (PDF)

Prefabrication

The building is designed to be assembled from precast elements. Move over the numbers to read each part.

  1. 1
    Structure of the private floors

    Precast slabs of 4 by 3.3 metres on a regular grid of columns.

  2. 2
    Beam grid at the transition floor

    A grid of beams collects the loads of the private floors above.

  3. 3
    Transition trees

    Branching supports that carry the housing floors over the open public floors.

  4. 4
    Structure of the public floors

    A wider frame that frees the ground floors for public use.

  5. 5
    Hollow-core slabs

    Precast hollow-core slabs span the public floors.

  6. 6
    Precast slabs

    The floor slabs of the private levels are placed as precast pieces.

  7. 7
    Railing and solar protection

    An industrialised railing that also works as solar protection on the balconies.

  8. 8
    Precast concrete facade

    Precast concrete panels close the public volume.

  9. 9
    Facade of precast elements

    Vertical precast fins give rhythm and shade to the lower floors.
