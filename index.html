Present

I’m an architect specialized in sustainable design, currently working at IDOM, where I help integrate technical innovation into architectural practice. Over the past years, I’ve contributed to numerous real-world façade projects, applying performance-based methodologies to optimize design processes and support decarbonization goals.

I hold a degree in Architecture from ETSAM (Universidad Politécnica de Madrid) and a Master’s in Architecture and Sustainability from the University of Navarra. I’m currently pursuing a practice-based PhD through a collaboration between IDOM, the University of Navarra, and the University of Florida.

My research explores how artificial intelligence can enhance early-stage façade design, leveraging data-informed strategies and simulation tools to enable more intelligent, efficient, and environmentally responsive decision-making from the very beginning of the creative process. My goal is to advance architectural workflows by integrating AI, simulation, and data-driven design into mainstream practice, fostering a new generation of buildings that are not only aesthetically compelling but also technically optimized and environmentally conscious.
