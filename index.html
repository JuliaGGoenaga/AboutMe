Present

I work at IDOM on sustainability and computational design, and I am part of its digital transformation group. In parallel I am doing a PhD between the Universidad de Navarra and the University of Florida, and I collaborate with universities on teaching and research.

  1. Sustainable architecture

    Daylight and solar radiation analysis and optimization, so that envelopes are designed with performance data from the first sketch.

    • Daylight and radiation analysis
    • Energy analysis
    • Facade design
    • Passive design strategies
    • Embodied carbon

  2. Computational design and AI

    Computational tools and AI models applied to design, and the digital transformation of design processes inside a large engineering and architecture firm.

    • Computational design tools
    • AI model training for design
    • Digital transformation at IDOM

  3. Integrated design and human-computer interaction

    Methods that bring sustainability into the earliest design phases, and simple interactive tools that help architects learn and decide.

    • Integrated design methods
    • Simple interactive tools
    • Training architects
